About this project

The Emotion Project asks volunteers to read short tweets and label the emotion most clearly expressed in each one. Your labels — combined with everyone else's — form a dataset researchers can use to study how emotion is expressed in informal, everyday text.

The six emotions

😠 Anger 😨 Fear 😄 Joy 🥰 Love 😢 Sadness 😲 Surprise

Where the tweets come from

The tweets in this task are drawn from the publicly available Emotion dataset (Saravia et al., 2018), hosted on Hugging Face at dair-ai/emotion. It contains English-language tweets originally labeled with one of six basic emotions.

A note on this kind of work

It's worth naming honestly: using AI or automated systems to classify human emotion from text is a genuinely debated practice — text alone often under-determines what someone actually feels, and automated emotion inference has real risks of misuse. This task exists to produce careful human judgments, and to make the difficulty of the task (ambiguity, missing context, multiple plausible readings) visible rather than papered over.

Saravia, E., Liu, H-C. T., Huang, Y-H., Wu, J., & Chen, Y-S. (2018). CARER: Contextualized Affect Representations for Emotion Recognition. Proceedings of EMNLP 2018.
